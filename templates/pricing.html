Meta WhatsApp API charges are billed separately

On the Growth, Scale and Custom plans, official Meta WhatsApp Business API conversation and template charges are set by Meta and billed directly to you — they're not part of the PravahAI subscription. When your 24-hour customer service window closes, approved message templates may apply. Meta's pricing and policies can change; PravahAI does not control these fees.
