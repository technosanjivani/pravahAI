What PravahAI does

Four channels. One context that never breaks.

Most tools bolt channels together and lose the thread between them. PravahAI keeps a single conversational memory flowing underneath every touchpoint — so a customer can start on WhatsApp, follow up by email, and finish on a call without repeating themselves.

WhatsApp

Template & session messaging with rich replies, built for two-way business conversation.

Email

Transactional and lifecycle email that reads the same context as every other channel.

Voice

Conversational IVR with live transcription — calls route with full history in hand.

Web Chat

Embed a live, context-aware assistant directly on your site in minutes.
