Simple steps to grow your business with AI — capture leads, reply automatically on WhatsApp, call customers, and book meetings, all without lifting a finger.

What is PravaahAI? START HERE

An all-in-one AI assistant for your business.

PravaahAI finds and manages your leads, talks to your customers on WhatsApp and phone calls automatically, and helps you book meetings — so you spend less time chasing people and more time closing deals.

Think of PravaahAI as a tireless sales assistant that never sleeps: it replies to WhatsApp messages, makes phone calls, sends emails, and books meetings — all by itself, using AI.

How It Works — The Big Picture

  1. Leads come in — added by you, imported from a file, or captured from your website/WhatsApp.
  2. Your AI Agent talks to them — on WhatsApp, on a phone call, or on your website's chat widget.
  3. The lead gets scored — PravaahAI marks them Cold, Warm, or Hot based on how the conversation goes.
  4. Meetings get booked automatically — if a lead is interested, the AI books a slot and sends reminders.
  5. You just review and close — check your dashboard, see who's hot, and follow up.

Setting Up Your Account

A few quick things to connect once, and you're set.

Step 1: Complete Your Profile

  1. Go to Profile in the sidebar.
  2. Add your business name, category (Real Estate / Share Market / Import-Export / Other), website and address.
  3. This helps the AI write better, more relevant messages for your leads.

Step 2: Connect a Messaging Channel

Go to Integrations and connect one WhatsApp provider — either Evolution API or Wirebase. This is how PravaahAI sends and receives WhatsApp messages for you.

Tip: You only need one active provider at a time. Switch anytime from the "Active WhatsApp Provider" toggle in Integrations.

Step 3: Connect Email (Optional)

Add your Gmail (with an app password) or Resend account if you want to send outreach emails as part of campaigns.

Step 4: Create Your AI Agent

Go to Eva Voice AI → Voice Agents and create one agent. This single agent can be turned ON for WhatsApp, Voice Calls, Web Widget, and Email — so your tone stays consistent everywhere.

Step 5: Pick a Plan

Go to My Plan to choose a subscription. Every plan includes a set number of "Eva Minutes" — the minutes your AI agent is allowed to spend on phone calls.

Understanding Lead Status

Every lead is automatically marked with a temperature, based on how the conversation is going:

  • 🕓 Pending — just added, no conversation yet
  • ❄️ Cold — not very interested
  • 🌤 Warm — showing some interest
  • 🔥 Hot — ready to buy or book
You can also change a lead's status yourself at any time from the dropdown next to their name.

Segments

Segments are simple folders for organizing leads — for example "Dubai Expo 2026" or "Cold Outreach Q1". Use them to filter leads and target specific groups with campaigns.

Useful Lead Actions

  • 📞 Call — instantly calls the lead using your voice agent
  • 💬 WhatsApp — opens the full chat history and lets you send a manual message
  • ✏️ Edit — update lead details or their "AI Task" (a specific goal for the bot)
  • 🔎 Scrape Emails — automatically visits each lead's website to fill in a missing email or description

WhatsApp Bot — Auto-Reply

PravaahAI's most-used feature — instant AI replies, day or night.

How It Works

  1. A lead sends a WhatsApp message to your connected number.
  2. PravaahAI checks who they are (or creates a new lead if they're new).
  3. Your AI Agent reads the conversation and its own instructions, then writes a natural reply.
  4. The lead's temperature is updated based on the conversation.
  5. The reply is sent back automatically, and logged in your Inbox.
Most leads go cold because nobody replies fast enough. PravaahAI replies within seconds, any time of day, so no message is ever left waiting.

The Inbox

Every WhatsApp conversation shows up here — you can read the full history, and reply manually any time. There's also a toggle to mute the AI for a specific lead, useful once a human takes over that chat.

Setting the Bot's Behavior

Your AI agent's system prompt (set under Eva Voice AI → Voice Agents → Role) controls its tone and personality. Each lead can also have its own AI Task — a specific goal like "convince them to book a demo".

Automation Campaigns

Multi-step outreach — WhatsApp, Email, Calls — built with drag-and-drop.

Building a Campaign

  1. Click New Campaign, give it a name.
  2. Drag steps onto the canvas: WhatsApp, Email, Call, Wait, or Condition.
  3. Click each step to set its message — or turn on Auto-generate with AI to let AI personalize the message for every lead.
  4. Connect the steps in order by dragging from one step's dot to the next.
  5. Assign leads to the campaign — filter by segment or status to pick the right group.
  6. Click Launch — now or scheduled for later.

Broadcast Campaigns

Sent once, to a fixed list of leads you picked. Messages go out in small batches over time so they don't look like spam.

Trigger Campaigns

Run automatically, forever, the moment something happens — a new lead comes in, a call ends, or a lead becomes Hot or Warm.

Instead of messaging every lead by hand, build the sequence once and let PravaahAI run it consistently for every new lead that fits.

Eva Voice AI — Phone Calls

PravaahAI's calling engine — natural spoken conversations, no human needed.

How a Call Works

  1. Eva dials the lead using your connected phone number (VaniSetu, VoiceLink, or Twilio).
  2. Your agent's opening line starts the call.
  3. The AI listens and responds naturally, following its system prompt.
  4. After the call, AI writes a summary, decides the outcome, and updates the lead's status.
  5. If a follow-up is needed, Eva schedules itself to call back automatically.

Eva Minutes

Every call uses "Eva Minutes" from your plan's balance. You can see how many minutes are left at the top of the Eva dashboard, and recharge any time from My Plan.

Auto-Call New Leads

Turn this on for an agent, and Eva will automatically call every new lead the moment they're added — respecting your chosen office hours.

Test Call & Text Chat Test

Before going live, use Test Call to try a real call to any number, or Text Chat Test to chat with your agent's personality in writing — no minutes used.

Web Widget — Chat on Your Website

Puts your AI agent right on your site as a chat bubble.

Setting It Up

  1. Go to Eva Voice AI → Web Widget.
  2. Click New Widget, pick the agent it should use, and choose a bubble color.
  3. Write a greeting message.
  4. Copy the embed code and paste it into your website's HTML.

Handy Options

  • Capture Leads — saves visitor details into your Leads list automatically
  • Ask for details first — collects name/phone before the chat starts
  • Auto-greet — the AI speaks first after a short delay, instead of waiting to be clicked

Meetings

Booked automatically — on WhatsApp, on a call, or manually by you.

Setting Your Availability

Go to Eva Voice AI → Meeting Templates and set your weekly available hours, meeting length, and Google Meet link. The AI only offers slots inside these hours.

How Automatic Booking Works

  1. The lead tells the AI (on WhatsApp or a call) they'd like a meeting.
  2. The AI asks for a date and time.
  3. PravaahAI checks it's inside your available hours and not already booked.
  4. If it's free, the meeting is booked and confirmed automatically.
  5. If it's not free, the AI offers the closest available alternatives.
10 minutes before any meeting, PravaahAI automatically sends a WhatsApp reminder to the lead and a notification to you.

Real Estate Tools

Extra tools unlocked when your business category is set to Real Estate.

Inventory

Add your property listings with photos, videos, and a brochure PDF. Each property gets its own shareable public page that you can send to any lead.

Site Visits

When a lead chats with your WhatsApp bot about a property, the AI asks for their budget and preferred area, shows matching listings, and books a site visit automatically. All requested visits appear on the Site Visits page for you to confirm.

Team Members

Invite your team so leads can be shared and worked on together.

  • Go to Team and click Invite Team Member. They'll receive their login by email.
  • When a lead replies for the first time, they are automatically assigned to a team member in round-robin order (evenly, one after another).
  • Team members only see leads assigned to them — owners see everything.
  • Team members cannot access Integrations or Settings.

Plans & Eva Minutes

Your subscription decides which features are unlocked and how many calling minutes you get.

Checking Your Plan

Go to My Plan to see your current plan, minutes used vs. remaining, and when it renews.

Recharging

  • Buy or Upgrade a Plan — pick a plan from the Available Plans list and pay securely online
  • Recharge Eva Minutes — top up extra calling minutes any time without changing your plan
If a feature says "Recharge Needed", it just means your plan has expired or you're out of Eva Minutes — recharge from My Plan to keep going.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Write a clear system prompt for your AI agent — the clearer the instructions, the better the conversations.
  • Use Segments to keep your leads organized before running a campaign.
  • Start with a Test Call or Text Chat Test before launching anything live.
  • Set office hours so leads aren't called at odd times.
  • Check the Inbox daily — even though the AI replies automatically, it's good to review important conversations.
  • Keep your Meeting Template up to date so the AI never offers a time you're not actually free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to reply to every WhatsApp message myself?

No — once your AI agent has WhatsApp turned on, it replies automatically. You can always jump in and reply manually any time.

What happens if I run out of Eva Minutes?

Voice calls will pause until you recharge. WhatsApp and Email automation are not affected.

Can one agent handle WhatsApp, calls, and my website widget at once?

Yes — one agent can be switched on for every channel at the same time, so your tone stays consistent everywhere.

Can I turn off the AI for one specific lead?

Yes — open that lead's conversation in the Inbox and turn off the "AI Auto-Reply" toggle.
